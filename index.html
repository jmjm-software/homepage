Profile

I'm Josh — a developer who thinks like an attacker and an attacker who ships like a developer. Most of my career I've built enterprise platforms end to end: architecture, frontend, backend, pipelines, production. The rest of the time I've been on the other side of the same systems — testing web applications, reviewing code, reversing firmware, and reporting what I find through responsible disclosure.

The two halves feed each other. Building a DeFi platform to $10M peak TVL with a lean team taught me what auditors actually look for; finding account takeovers and data exposure in production systems taught me how to build so those classes of bugs never make it out the door.
